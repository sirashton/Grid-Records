      About the data

      Generation comes from the IESO, the Independent Electricity System Operator, which runs Ontario's grid and wholesale market. Its Generator Output by Fuel Type Hourly report gives the output of every generator connected to the transmission grid, summed by fuel, each hour from 1 January 2015. The hours since the latest yearly file come from the Generator Output and Capability report, which the IESO rewrites each hour; once a day is over its figures match the yearly file exactly.

      Solar and wind connected to local distribution networks are not in these reports. That is most of Ontario's solar: the IESO counts it as lower demand, so the Solar figures here are only the large solar farms. Batteries are the IESO's fuel type OTHER, which is only battery storage, and only their discharge is published. Imports and exports are not in the generation data. Net exports here are inferred: transmission-connected generation less the IESO's Ontario Demand, which also counts losses on the grid.

      Low carbon here is nuclear, hydro, wind, solar and biofuel. Gas is Ontario's only fossil fuel: its last coal plant, Thunder Bay, stopped burning coal in 2014, before these files begin.

      A spell is a run of consecutive hours that meet the threshold. Gaps in the data of up to an hour are bridged when the hours either side qualify; longer gaps end the spell. The IESO's market hours are Eastern Standard Time all year; years and dates here are Toronto time, and hour-of-day charts use Eastern Standard Time.
