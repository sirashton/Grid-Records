      About the data

      Generation comes from ADME, which administers Uruguay's wholesale electricity market, through its generation by source page. ADME calls the figures approximate values from the grid's SCADA system. They are hourly, in Uruguay time, from 2018: the output of the four big hydro plants (Salto Grande, and Bonete, Baygorria and Palmar on the Río Negro), wind, solar, thermal and biomass, imports from Argentina and Brazil, and demand. Rooftop panels are not counted.

      Salto Grande is shared with Argentina; the figure is the output ADME reports for Uruguay. Exports are not published in this series, so they are inferred: what generation and imports leave over after demand. They also carry the grid's losses, so they are approximate.

      Renewables here are wind, hydro, solar and biomass (mostly wood waste and black liquor burned at pulp mills). Fossil fuels are the thermal plants, which burn gas oil, diesel and fuel oil.

      A spell is a run of consecutive hours that meet the threshold. Gaps in the data of up to an hour are bridged when the hours either side qualify; longer gaps end the spell. Years and hours are in Uruguay time (UTC-3).
