    Battery units

    Elexon has no battery fuel type, so units are matched by name (battery, BESS, storage) or by National Grid's naming convention for battery units, where the unit both imports and exports. Batteries connected to local networks outside the Balancing Mechanism are not visible in any of these feeds.

    Where battery power comes from

    Batteries don't make electricity, they move it in time, so their output is only as clean as what charged them. This treats the whole fleet as one store that empties first in, first out: energy taken in each half hour is tagged with that half hour's grid mix, and comes back out in the order it went in. It's an experiment, so it only runs when you ask.

    How the attribution works, and its limits

      📚 About the data

      Generation by fuel comes from NESO's historic generation mix, half-hourly since 2009 and updated hourly. It includes wind and solar on local networks, which NESO estimates. Fossil fuels here are gas and coal; oil is too small to report separately and sits in "Other".

      Battery data comes from Elexon's Insights Solution: each battery unit's Physical Notifications (its planned output, published in real time) and its B1610 metered output (published about five days later). NESO also counts battery discharge inside its "Other" category, and plans to publish batteries separately.

      A spell is a run of consecutive half hours that meet the threshold. Gaps in the data of up to an hour are bridged when the half hours either side qualify; longer gaps end the spell.
