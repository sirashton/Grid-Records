        About the data

        The figures are the supply and demand results (エリア需給実績) that each area's transmission operator publishes: Hokkaido, Tohoku, TEPCO (Tokyo), Chubu, Hokuriku, Kansai, Chugoku, Shikoku and Kyushu. This page adds the nine areas together. Okinawa, the tenth area, is not included: its grid is not connected to the other nine, and its operator's site refuses automated requests, so "Japan" here means the nine interconnected areas, which carry nearly all of the country's demand.

        Since April 2024 every operator publishes average output for each half hour by source: nuclear, LNG, coal, oil and other thermal, hydro, geothermal, biomass, solar and wind, pumped storage and batteries, with the solar and wind output each operator ordered off. Before that, each published hourly figures in its own layout from April 2016, with thermal plants as one total. So fossil output here runs from April 2016, and the split by fuel from April 2024; an hourly reading counts for both of its half hours. A half hour counts only when all nine areas have published it, so July to September 2018 (Hokkaido's figures for that quarter are only published as a spreadsheet this site doesn't read) and February 2024 (Kansai published neither layout for that month) are gaps.

        Fossil fuels are all thermal plants: LNG, coal, oil and other thermal. Renewables are hydro, solar, wind, geothermal and biomass. Low carbon adds nuclear. Pumped storage counts as generation when it runs; pumping, battery charging and curtailed output are separate measures, not counted in generation. Flows between the nine areas cancel out in the total and are not shown.

        A spell is a run of consecutive half hours that meet the threshold. Gaps in the data of up to an hour are bridged when the half hours either side qualify; longer gaps end the spell. Years run in Japan time.

        Built by Alex, and the source code is on GitHub.
