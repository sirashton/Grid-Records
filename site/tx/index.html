      About the data

      Generation comes from ERCOT, the Electric Reliability Council of Texas, which runs the grid for about 90% of the state's demand. El Paso, the Panhandle's northern edge, parts of East Texas and a few other areas are on other grids and are not included. The history is ERCOT's Interval Generation by Fuel reports, settlement data every 15 minutes from 2007, published about once a month. The days since the latest report come from ERCOT's live fuel mix dashboard, averaged into quarter hours; it keeps only two days, so a short gap can show between the last report and the dashboard until the next report fills it.

      ERCOT's "Other" is shown as Batteries and other: it includes battery discharge, which is nearly all of it in recent years, as well as petroleum coke and oil. Battery charging is not counted, and nor is generation that factories and refineries make and use on site. Gas combines ERCOT's combined-cycle and simple-cycle figures. Rooftop solar is not included. The dashboard folds biomass into Other, so biomass reads zero for the latest days.

      Renewables here are wind, solar, hydro and biomass. Low carbon adds nuclear. Fossil fuels are gas and coal.

      A spell is a run of consecutive quarter hours that meet the threshold. Gaps in the data of up to an hour are bridged when the quarter hours either side qualify; longer gaps end the spell. Years run in Central time.
