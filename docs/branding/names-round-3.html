Name ideas, round three

Fourteen new names, every one rated High: a web search for the exact string found no established owner. About thirty candidates were checked and the ones that turned out Medium or Low (Gridrun, Quietgrid, Gridfirm, Cleanspell, Fossilfall, Firmhold and others) were dropped. Each card has the lockup, the tab mock, a sentence on fit, the search check, and a note on what the word means in German, Polish or Māori. Domain availability was not checked; nothing in the searches suggested an obvious .com in use for any of them.

Duration and records

How long the grid keeps going, and the records it sets.

Gridstreak
The longest streaks without coal and gas, and the one running now.
Gridstreak
New tab
+

The most literal of the set: the grid, and a streak that is still counting. Easy to say and spell, obviously about electricity, and it carries the live feel of the records. Weakness: "streak" is a little sporty, and it is the least poetic name here.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are a Grid-Stick kit for microscopes and an old arcade game, Grid Seeker. The exact string is free.

Languages: Not a word in German, Polish or Māori (Māori has no s or d, so none of the names on this page can be Māori words).

Gridlast
How long the grid lasts without fossil fuel.
Gridlast
New tab
+

Grid plus the question the site answers: how long does it last? Short and plain. Weakness: a German reader sees "Last" as load (Netzlast is grid load), which is apt but shifts the meaning.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Only data-grid API documentation ("last row", "last cell"). Free.

Languages: German: Last means load or burden, so Gridlast reads as "grid load", which is harmless and even fitting. Nothing in Polish.

Burnlow
Running the grid on the lowest burn of coal and gas yet.
Burnlow
New tab
+

Low burn is exactly what the records measure, turned into a word that sounds like a Scottish village (a burn is a stream). Pleasant to say, memorable, and it travels. Weakness: without the tagline it could be read as a fire-safety setting.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are Ordnance Survey name books and places like Burnwell and Brownlow. Free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish; in Scots a burn is a stream, so it sounds like a place name rather than anything unfortunate.

Reliability and calm

A grid that holds steady with nothing burning.

Stillgrid
A grid that runs steady and quiet without coal and gas.
Stillgrid
New tab
+

Still as in calm and as in continuing: the grid still running, with the turbines still. A reliability name without the word reliable. Weakness: "still" can also read as stuck, and it is the most abstract of the set.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are unrelated grid companies (Plexigrid, Withthegrid) and dictionary pages. Free.

Languages: German: still means quiet or silent, which is a bonus. Nothing in Polish.

Clearburn
Clear of coal and gas: how long the grid stays that way.
Clearburn
New tab
+

Reads like Clearwater: a run of hours clear of burning, said in one calm word. Weakness: it can be misread as a clean kind of burning, and it shares the Scottish stream-name sound with Burnlow.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Only Ordnance Survey name-book pages and a village called Clearbrook. Free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish.

Noburn
The hours when the grid burns nothing, and the records they set.
Noburn
New tab
+

The bluntest name on the page: no burn. Two syllables, impossible to misspell, and it works whatever the fuel. Weakness: it sounds like a sunscreen or a stove brand before it sounds like a grid.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are places called Naburn and Newburn and a surname. Free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish; Naburn is a village near York, which is the nearest thing to a clash.

The end of coal and gas

Words for a fleet that has stopped burning.

Emberless
Nothing left burning on the grid, and how long it stays that way.
Emberless
New tab
+

The best-sounding name of the round: a real-looking word nobody uses, soft but exact, meaning no embers, nothing burning. Travels to coal and gas alike. Weakness: it says the end state, not the records or the grid, so it leans on the tagline.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Only dictionary and crossword pages for "ember". The exact word is free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish. Note that Ember, the energy think tank, publishes grid data on this same transition, so Emberless could be read as a nod to them. Ember.js shares the root but not the word.

Unfuelled
The grid running unfuelled: no coal, no gas, counted every half hour.
Unfuelled
New tab
+

A real English word used freshly for a grid with nothing fed into it. Quiet, confident, and the British spelling keeps it ownable. Weakness: Americans will search "unfueled", and it needs the tagline on first meeting.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Dictionary entries and aviation documents using "non-fueled". Free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish.

Unstoked
Fires unstoked: how long the grid runs with nobody feeding the boilers.
Unstoked
New tab
+

Stoking is what you do to a coal or gas fire, so unstoked is the grid when nobody is doing it. Vivid, industrial, and it travels. Weakness: "stoked" means excited to a younger reader, which makes this sound like a mood.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are a video game and a film called Stoked. The exact word is free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish.

Fuelfall
The fall of coal and gas, measured every half hour.
Fuelfall
New tab
+

Built like nightfall and rainfall: the slow fall of fuel burning on the grid, in one easy word, and it works in every country because it does not name the fuel. Weakness: it could be heard as fuel prices falling.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are fuel-price and aircraft fuel-tank news. Free.

Languages: German: Fall means case or fall, which is harmless. Nothing in Polish.

Lastfossil
Counting down the last fossil fuel on the grid.
Lastfossil
New tab
+

Last Burn with the fuel named generically, so it works in Britain, Germany, Poland and Australia without changing. Weakness: three syllables heavier than Last Burn, and "fossil" pulls toward dinosaurs.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Results are geology formations and a palaeontology article. Free.

Languages: Not a word in German or Polish (German uses fossil as an adjective, so it reads naturally there).

Dimburn
Coal and gas burning dimmer every year, and the records as they fade.
Dimburn
New tab
+

The burn dimming rather than ending: honest about where most grids are, and it sounds like a place. Weakness: dim also means slow-witted, and to a Polish ear dym (smoke) is close.

Findability: High

Search check. No owner. Only Scottish place-name records and a Fife fire incident. Free.

Languages: Polish: dym means smoke, pronounced almost the same, so Dimburn sounds like "smoke-burn" there. Not a word in German.

New tab

The endpoint in one word: zero burn. Easy to say, obviously a measure, and it is the headline the site will one day write. Weakness: zero-anything is crowded territory in energy branding, and it sounds a little like a diet product.

Nilburn
Nil coal, nil gas: the hours the grid burns nothing.
Nilburn
New tab
+

The British version of Zeroburn: nil, as in a score. Shorter, drier, and it sounds like a village. Weakness: nil reads as a scoreline first, and the word is less obvious to non-British readers.

Verdict

Top three across all rounds

  1. Longspell. Still first. Built on the site's own unit, clean in search, one word that looks like a name in the tab, and it travels because a spell is a spell whatever the fuel. It has the editorial feel Alex liked in The Long Run without the search problem.
  2. Emberless. The best new word of this round: soft, exact, unique, and it describes the end state the whole site is counting toward. Works for coal and gas alike. Needs the tagline on first meeting, then it sticks.
  3. Gridstreak. The safest High: it says grid and it says record run, nobody can misspell it, and nothing owns it. Less poetic than the other two, which is the trade.

If findability can bend to Medium, Last Burn remains the strongest story name; Unfuelled and Stillgrid are the quiet alternatives if Emberless feels too soft.
