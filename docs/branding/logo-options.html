Names

Six names, each shown as a lockup next to the two strongest square marks (3c Monogram and 3d Stacked monogram) with a one-line tagline. The planned home is grid.gathered.consulting, so the name does not have to carry the domain.

Grid Records
How long the grid runs without fossil fuels, and the records it keeps setting.
Grid Records
How long the grid runs without fossil fuels, and the records it keeps setting.

The incumbent, and it still says exactly what the site is: a record book for the grid. Plain, searchable, and it survives the move to New Zealand and Germany without edits.

The Long Run
Record spells without fossil fuels, across the grids that are getting there.
The Long Run
Record spells without fossil fuels, across the grids that are getting there.

Doubles as the thing being measured (how long a run lasts) and the direction of travel (the long run of the transition). Memorable without being cute.

Spells
How long each grid can run on little or no fossil fuel.
Spells
How long each grid can run on little or no fossil fuel.

The site's own word for its unit of measure, and the shortest option here. Reads a little bare on its own, so it leans on the tagline.

Grid Streaks
The longest streaks without fossil fuels, and the ones happening now.
Grid Streaks
The longest streaks without fossil fuels, and the ones happening now.

A streak is what people already call an unbroken run, and it carries the live, still-counting feel of the records. Slightly sporty for a data site.

Crossover
Where low-carbon overtakes fossil, grid by grid, and for how long.
Crossover
Where low-carbon overtakes fossil, grid by grid, and for how long.

Names the moment the marks draw: the lines crossing. Strong next to the X monogram, but it describes the transition more than the records.

Off Gas
How long Britain's grid runs without gas, and the records along the way.
Off Gas
How long Britain's grid runs without gas, and the records along the way.

The bluntest and most quotable, and the right name for the UK story. It fits New Zealand and Germany less well, where the fossil fuel in question is not always gas.

The New record family (1, 1a–1c) from the last round is not shown here; its staged build and final hop are what the new marks above borrow.
